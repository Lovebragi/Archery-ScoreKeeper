射箭计分宝

ARCHERY SCOREKEEPER PRO · 使用指南

快速开始

1. 开机后主页显示五个入口：开始训练、历史记录、日期时间、设置、联网。用上/下选择（首尾循环），按确定进入。

2. 「日期时间」直接从主页进入，按上/下调整年/月/日/时/分，按确定逐项确认，最后一项保存后回到主页；长按确定随时保存返回。

3. 「设置」里可以改靶子类型（40 半靶 / 三联靶 / 40 全靶 / 80 全靶 / 122 全靶）、屏幕方向、亮度、息屏时间、是否记录内十、语音输入开关、提示音量和是否保存 Wi-Fi 密码。设置页一屏显示 6 行，光标移出窗口会自动翻页，顶部显示当前范围（例如 1-6 / 10）。设置项包括靶子类型、屏幕方向、亮度、息屏时间、记录内十、每组计时、倒数十秒语音、语音输入、提示音量和保存 Wi-Fi 密码。

- 提示音量有 0/20/40/60/80/100% 六档，0% 为静音；每调一档会立即响一声试听。

- 把「语音输入」设为关，录入页就只保留按键操作。

- 「保存Wi-Fi密码」默认开启；关闭会清除已保存网络，并停止开机自动重连。

4. 回主页选「开始训练」，确认组数和每组箭数（默认 6 组 × 6 支）；三联靶还要分配上靶/中靶/下靶各几支，三者之和等于每组箭数。按确定开始。

5. 录入页按上/下调整当前环值的箭数，按确定换到下一环。合计凑满本组箭数时会跳到提交行，再按一次确定提交；小结页按确定进入下一组，全部完成后自动保存。

6. 语音报靶（可选）：

a. 保持「语音输入」为开。

b. 按住上键约 0.2 秒，听到「滴」后开始说，松手等待结果。例如「十环三根，九环两根，脱靶一个」。

c. 也可以只报一部分，例如「三根九环」，屏幕会提示「还差 3 支」；接着说「全都是九」或「剩下都是八」可以补齐剩余的箭。

d. 口语说法「仨十」「俩脱靶」「三根儿九环」「俩十，一九，俩八」都能识别；常见同音字会自动纠正。

e. 识别失败会响两声低音并提示重说；也可以随时改用按键录入。

f. 单次录音最长 8 秒，需要设备已连接 2.4GHz Wi-Fi。

7. 语音需要自备「豆包流式语音识别模型 2.0」账号（新账号赠送 20 小时免费时长）：

a. 打开 https://www.volcengine.com/ 注册并登录；注册后需用微信或抖音扫码，并通过摄像头完成实名认证，未实名无法开通语音服务。

b. 打开 https://console.volcengine.com/speech/service/10038 ，开通「豆包流式语音识别模型 2.0」。

c. 在页面创建或选择应用，记下 APPID 与 Access Token（Token 可以重置，只填在自己的设备上，不要发到公开渠道）。

8. 用手机把凭据填进设备：

a. 设备进入「联网 → 手机配网 / 导出」，屏幕会显示二维码和热点 Archery-Setup。

b. 手机连接 Archery-Setup，打开 http://192.168.4.1/ 。

c. 在「豆包直连凭据」里填 APPID 和 Access Token，点「保存直连凭据」；Token 输入框不会回显。

d. 在同一页选择家里的 2.4GHz Wi-Fi 并连接，回到录入页即可使用语音。

9. Wi-Fi 记忆：连接成功的网络会保存在设备里（最多 10 个，同网络更新密码）。之后在「联网 → 扫描网络」里选中已保存的网络，按确定直接连接，不必再输密码；手机配网页里对已保存网络留空密码也能连。密码以可读形式保存在设备本地，拿到设备的人可以读取；如果设备会借出或转手，建议在设置里关闭「保存Wi-Fi密码」。

10. 历史记录：主页进入「历史记录」，上/下移动光标，确定查看某一组的详情；按住上/下可快速滚动，首尾循环；长按确定直接回主页，双击确定切换「全部 / 今天 / 最近 7 天」。

11. 导出记录：设备进入「联网 → 手机配网 / 导出」，手机连 Archery-Setup 后打开 http://192.168.4.1/ ，点「下载训练记录（CSV）」，文件名可直接用 Excel 打开。

12. 联网与对时：在「联网」页选择家里的 2.4GHz Wi-Fi 并输入密码，或在手机上用配网页填写；连接后可在「日期时间」里手动校准，也可用「立即对时」通过 NTP 校准。

13. 省电：息屏时间到后会自动关屏并浅睡，任意键唤醒并回到原页面，训练进度不会丢。停留在联网页或配网页 2 分钟无按键只会关闭背光，热点和页面保持，按任意键恢复。

14. 比赛计时：在设置里把「每组计时」设为 20/30/40 秒每支（关=不计时）。按开始后先静默 1 秒、两声提示、10 秒准备（结束一声），随后按「每组箭数 × 每支秒数」倒计时；剩 30 秒响 C 大调琶音，归零三声后进入记录页。倒计时最后 10 秒有女声报数，可在设置里关闭「倒数十秒语音」。

15. 首页名称：手机配网页底部「首页名称」可输入 1～8 个字符并保存，设备首页标题立即更新；默认「射箭计分宝」。

Quick start

1. The home screen has five entries: Start training, History, Date/time, Settings and Network. Move with up/down (wrapping at both ends) and press OK to enter.

2. Date/time opens straight from the home screen: adjust year/month/day/hour/minute with up/down, confirm each field with OK, and the last field saves and returns home; a long OK press saves and returns at any time.

3. Settings holds the target type (40 cm half, three-spot half, 40/80/122 cm full), screen orientation, brightness, screen-off time, whether to record the inner ten, voice input, beep volume and whether to save Wi-Fi passwords. The page shows six rows at a time and scrolls when the cursor leaves the window, with the current range shown at the top (for example 1-6 / 10). Rows are target type, orientation, brightness, screen-off time, record inner ten, group timer, ten-second countdown voice, voice input, beep volume and Wi-Fi password storage.

- Beep volume has six levels, 0/20/40/60/80/100%; 0% is mute and each change plays one preview beep.

- With voice input off the entry screen is keypad-only.

- "Save Wi-Fi passwords" is on by default; turning it off clears the saved networks and stops auto-reconnect at boot.

4. Back on the home screen pick Start training and confirm the group count and arrows per group (6 groups of 6 by default). For the three-spot target split the arrows across the upper, middle and lower faces; the three counts must add up to the group size. Press OK to start.

5. On the entry screen use up/down to set the arrow count for the highlighted ring and OK to move to the next ring. When the counts add up, OK jumps to the commit row and a second press submits the group. The summary takes you to the next group; the session saves automatically when you finish.

6. Voice scoring (optional):

a. Keep Voice input on.

b. Hold the up button for about 0.2 s, wait for the beep, then speak and release. For example: "ten ring three, nine ring two, one miss".

c. You may report part of the end, for example "three nines"; the screen says how many arrows remain. Then say "all nines" or "the rest are eights" to fill the rest.

d. Casual forms such as "sa tens", "two misses", erhua endings and number-only pairs are understood; common homophones are corrected automatically.

e. A failed recognition plays two low beeps and asks you to repeat; you can always switch back to the keypad.

f. One recording is capped at 8 seconds and the device must be connected to a 2.4 GHz Wi-Fi network.

7. Voice needs your own Doubao streaming speech model 2.0 account (new accounts get 20 free hours):

a. Register and verify at https://www.volcengine.com/ . Verification scans a WeChat or Douyin QR code and uses the camera; unverified accounts cannot enable speech services.

b. Open https://console.volcengine.com/speech/service/10038 and enable "Doubao streaming speech model 2.0".

c. Create or pick an application on that page and note its APPID and Access Token. The token can be reset; keep it on your own device and never post it publicly.

8. Put the credentials on the device from a phone:

a. On the device open Network → Phone setup / export; it shows a QR code and the Archery-Setup hotspot.

b. Join Archery-Setup from the phone and open http://192.168.4.1/ .

c. Under "Doubao credentials" enter the APPID and Access Token and tap Save; the token field is never echoed back.

d. On the same page pick your home 2.4 GHz Wi-Fi and connect, then return to the entry screen.

9. Wi-Fi memory: each successful network is kept on the device (up to 10, with the same SSID updating its password). Pick a saved network under Network → Scan and press OK to connect without retyping the password; on the phone setup page you can also leave the password blank for a saved network. Passwords are stored in readable form on the device and can be read by anyone with it; if the device is lent out or resold, turn off "Save Wi-Fi passwords" in Settings.

10. History: open History, move with up/down and press OK to see a group's detail. Hold up/down to scroll quickly with wrap-around; a long OK press returns home and a double OK press switches All / Today / Last 7 days.

11. Export: open Network → Phone setup / export, join Archery-Setup from the phone and open http://192.168.4.1/ , then tap "Download training log (CSV)". The file opens directly in Excel.

12. Network and clock: pick your home 2.4 GHz Wi-Fi on the Network screen and type the password, or enter it on the phone page. Adjust the clock by hand under Date/time, or use "Sync now" for NTP.

13. Power: after the screen-off time the display turns off and the device enters light sleep; any key wakes it back to the same screen without losing the session. On the network or setup page, two minutes without input only turns the backlight off while the hotspot and page stay alive; any key restores it.

14. Match timer: in Settings set Group timer to 20, 30 or 40 seconds per arrow (off disables it). Start pauses for one second, plays two beeps and gives a ten-second preparation (one beep at its end), then counts down arrows x seconds per arrow. The last 30 seconds play a C-major arpeggio and three beeps at zero open the recording page. A female voice counts the final ten seconds; turn off Ten-second countdown voice in Settings to disable it.

15. Home title: the Home name field at the bottom of the phone setup page accepts 1-8 characters and updates the device title immediately. The default is 「射箭计分宝」.

NFC：碰一碰打开本页。设备为被动 NTAG213，手机读取，设备不读取标签。
